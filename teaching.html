Teaching Philosophy

I aim to make economics accessible and engaging by blending theory with data-driven applications. My approach emphasizes:

Clarity and structure

Step-by-step coverage of econometric tools, building intuition before formalism.

Active learning

Problem-solving and applied examples with real datasets in every unit.

Supportive mentorship

Building confidence for students with limited prior exposure to quantitative methods.
